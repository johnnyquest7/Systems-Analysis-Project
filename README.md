# Systems-Analysis-Project

DriverPass is a fictional company that was used as a vehicle to train on system analysis and design.
The backstory is that DriverPass wants to provide lessons and practice materials for student drivers. 
They hired the team out to design an online system that can provide written tests, schedule practice 
sessions with real instructors, and store all of the needed information for the DriverPass team.

Whilst I feel like I did a decent job explaining what the requirements would be for the project, I 
also would have revised a couple of things. For one, the UML diagrams I made could be better organized.
Also, some of the diagrams could have been more detailed. I still think that anyone could understand
the general design choices I made, but this does seem to be the kind of work where you can iterate 
endlessly if you wanted to.
The needs of the client were rather straight forward. They had a clear goal in mind. This made the 
design process easy in many ways, but there is always a choice you make about what gets prioritized.
In the case of this project, we needed to make sure we were interpreting the clients needs correctly
because there was no asking for clarification. In real life settings, this may be the case for a design team 
as well. You have to make sure you are considering all the things the client wants, but also what 
is realistic to implement. Information gathering is the most crucial step.
